    Read only the big words. The faint line underneath is the whole sentence, so you know where your piece sits: if it runs straight into a name, leave it hanging; if it ends a sentence, land it. Names and places are said mid sentence. Silence before and after is trimmed and every take is levelled for you, so just leave a breath either side.
